Stay safe out there ★

Casual fun is better when everyone gets home safe. Read these before you meet anyone.

  • Meet in public first. Bar, restaurant, coffee shop — somewhere with people around. Never a private home for a first meet.
  • Tell a friend. Share who you're meeting, where, and when. Check in with them during and after.
  • Keep your own ride. Drive yourself or have your own way home. Don't depend on your date for transport.
  • Watch your drink. Don't leave it unattended, and don't accept drinks you didn't see poured.
  • Keep personal details private at first. No home address, no workplace, no financial info until real trust is built.
  • Video chat before meeting. A quick video call confirms they are who their photos say they are.
  • Trust your gut. If something feels off, leave. No explanation owed, no second chances required.
  • Protection, always. Bring your own and use it. No excuses, no exceptions.
  • Consent is everything. Only yes means yes — and anyone can change their mind at any time.
  • Money red flags. Anyone asking for money, gift cards, or "help with a bill" is a scammer. This site is 100% free — nobody here should ever ask you to pay for anything.

See something wrong?

Use the Report button on any profile or chat. Reports go straight to the site owner for review, and fake or abusive accounts get banned. You can also Block anyone — they'll never see you again.
